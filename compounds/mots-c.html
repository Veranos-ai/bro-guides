MOTS-c

A peptide of 16 amino acids that is encoded in mitochondrial DNA, the small set of genes inside the cell's energy-producing structures.

1 min read
Quick Answer

A peptide of 16 amino acids that is encoded in mitochondrial DNA, the small set of genes inside the cell's energy-producing structures.

Key Areas of Research

Published research on MOTS-c has looked at the following areas. The label shows the type of study.

  • Animal study Glucose handling and insulin sensitivity in mice.
  • Human study How levels of the peptide change with exercise, measured in samples from people.
  • Cell study Cell signalling linked to energy use, such as the AMPK pathway.
  • Animal study Ageing-related changes in mice.

What Is Not Known

The compound was described relatively recently. Studies in which people were given it are very limited, so most findings are from cells and animals.
