Retatrutide

An experimental peptide that copies three natural hormones, with promising results reported from human trials so far. The evidence is still early.

Studied For Weight Loss Fat Burning Metabolic Health Diabetes

What Is It

Quick Answer

Retatrutide is an experimental peptide, a lab-made messenger molecule, that copies three natural hormones.

Peptides are short chains of amino acids, the building blocks of proteins. Some work as hormones, the body's chemical messengers. Retatrutide copies three of them, which researchers link to appetite, blood sugar and how the body uses energy.

GLP-1Linked to feeling full and to blood sugar control
GIPWorks alongside GLP-1 on blood sugar and fat storage
GlucagonLinked to how much energy the body burns
Made ByEli Lilly
In TrialsOnce a week
StatusInvestigational, not FDA approved

How Strong Is the Evidence

Promising so far, but still early. The results come from different kinds of source, and they are not equally strong.

  1. Human studyPhase 2 trials

    Peer reviewed and published. Hundreds of adults, up to 48 weeks.

  2. Human studyPhase 3 trials

    Thousands of adults, 80 weeks. So far only sponsor topline releases, not peer reviewed.

  3. Common practiceCommunity reports

    Researchers sharing what they see. Self-reported and unverified, so the weakest evidence.

The Weekly Amounts, Explained

Trials gave each group its own weekly amount, so they could be compared. Here is what each amount was in the trials.

  • 0.5 to 1 mg

    The lowest weekly amounts tested. They appeared only in the phase 2 trials.

  • 2 mg

    Where every group began in the phase 3 trials, before stepping up.

  • 4 mg

    The lowest steady amount in the phase 3 trials.

  • 8 to 9 mg

    The middle groups: 8 mg in phase 2 and 9 mg in phase 3.

  • 12 mg

    The highest weekly amount tested, in both phase 2 and phase 3.

Starting Low and Stepping Up

In the phase 3 trials every group started at 2 mg a week and stepped up every 4 weeks. This is the path of the highest group. Other groups stopped at 4 or 9 mg.
