Key Areas of Research

Published research on BPC-157 has looked at the following areas. The label shows the type of study.

  • Animal study Tendon, ligament and muscle injury models in rats and mice.
  • Animal study Injury and inflammation models of the gut in rodents.
  • Cell study Growth of new blood vessels (angiogenesis) in cell and tissue models.

What Is Not Known

Nearly all of the published work is in animals, and much of it comes from a small number of research groups. Controlled human data are very limited.
