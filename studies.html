Studies

One short summary for each published study, with the design, the numbers and the limits stated.

In progress Each summary is checked against the original paper before it appears here. The first set is being prepared.

How each summary is labelled

LabelWhat it means
Cell studyA laboratory study on cells or tissue.
Animal studyA study in animals. The species is named.
Human studyA study in people. The size and type are stated.
ReviewA paper that summarises several studies.

What every summary contains

  1. Quick answer. What was studied and what was found, in one or two lines.
  2. At a glance. The design, the subjects, the compound and what was measured.
  3. What they did. The methods as the authors published them.
  4. What they found. The reported results, with numbers.
  5. Limits. The size, the species and what was not tested.
  6. Citation. The full reference, with a link to the paper.
