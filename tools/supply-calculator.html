    How it works Total = sum of all vial masses. Used per week = amount per dose × days per week. Weeks = total ÷ used per week. Months = weeks × 7 ÷ 30.4. This is arithmetic only, so it ignores any amount lost when drawing. Check any result by hand before relying on it.
    Mixed vials and time The calculator counts milligrams, not shelf life. A mixed vial is commonly kept for about 30 days, and up to 8 weeks for robust sequences. Common practice The storage guide has the full table.
