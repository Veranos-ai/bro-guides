How to read this site

This is a resource for research use only.

We are simply reporting what other researchers have found, what published studies have indicated, and the practices researchers commonly follow. Every finding is labelled, so the reader can see where it comes from.

Nothing here is medical advice, and nothing here tells anyone what to do with a compound.

Labels

Where a statement comes from

Every finding on this site carries one of these labels.

LabelWhat it means
Cell studyA published laboratory study on cells or tissue.
Animal studyA published study in animals. The species is always named.
Human studyA published study in people. The size and type of study are stated.
ReviewA published paper that summarises several studies.
Common practice Something many researchers commonly do or report. It is not from a controlled study.
