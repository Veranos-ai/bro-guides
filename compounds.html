Compounds

One profile per compound, gathering what the studies report and the facts needed to handle it.

In progress A profile is written only once its sources have been checked. The first profiles follow the first study summaries.

What every profile contains

  1. Quick answer. What the compound is and its regulatory status, in one sentence.
  2. Basics. Class, origin, size and status in a short table.
  3. What the research covers. Grouped by evidence type, each line linking to its study summary.
  4. What is not known. The gaps, stated plainly.
  5. Handling notes. Storage and mixing facts, each labelled as common practice or taken from a data sheet.
  6. Key papers. The references behind the profile.
